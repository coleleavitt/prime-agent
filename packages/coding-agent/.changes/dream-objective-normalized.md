- Made the Dream-RSI replay objective scale-invariant: quality is normalized to the frozen pool's observed score range, the probe penalty is a fraction of the per-rollout budget `W * k1`, and the parallelism bonus is the mean batch fill, so `beta1`/`beta2` mean "fraction of a full-quality point". The previous raw-score defaults (0.01 per probe) charged a 36-probe circle-packing rollout 0.36 against real score gains of about 0.1, and the dreamer learned to stop exploring (probes 36 -> 1, best frozen at round 1) while the fixed-exploration control kept improving; the new defaults (`beta1 = beta2 = 0.05`) are fixed by replaying the recorded runs and `dream experiment` now records the objective it ran with and appends an `objective: normalized` note.
- Added a no-quality-regression guard to `selectBestPolicy`: a candidate whose mean replay quality falls below the current policy's (within `qualityEps`, default 0) is ineligible regardless of its objective value, so a policy can no longer win by collapsing exploration. `dream.dream` gains `dream.chosen_quality`, `dream.current_quality` and `dream.quality_rejected`; `dream improve` prints them.
- Added `--beta1` / `--beta2` to `prime-agent dream` (finite, non-negative; `loop`, `experiment`, `improve` and `replay`), and `replay`/`improve` now measure the cost term against `--k1`.
- Fixed a wall-clock leak into the rollout rng: each attempt's fork was labelled with the node id, which embeds the tree id's clock milliseconds, so two `dream experiment --seed N` invocations grew different trees from the first probe. Attempt forks are now labelled by round, parent seq and child slot, so a seed fixes every score, shape and round table while the clock reaches only the on-disk ids. Recorded trees replay unchanged; new rollouts of an existing seed draw a different (now reproducible) stream.
