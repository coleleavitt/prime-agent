- Added a reason to every refine (`manual`, `refine_run`, `recurrence`, `regression`, `turn_interval`, `compact`, `rollback`), recorded on the refinement events in harness state and preserved by kernel-side harness saves.
- Changed a failure-triggered refine (recurrence or regression) that the judge finds addresses none of its failures to be rejected as `reject_unclaimed`; an agent `refine.run` merged into one is gated as directed instead.
- Changed a directed or checkpoint refine that claims no failure fingerprint to apply its edits without advancing RAVO state or opening a provisional window.
- Changed the RAVO gate to hold a failure-triggered refine only to the fingerprints that queued it, and any other refine only to the fingerprints of a failure refine merged into it plus failures that recur in its own session's ledger and were seen within the last 20 assistant turns of its branch (a failure last seen at a later turn than the branch has reached, as after a rewind, does not count). A fingerprint that queued the refine but does not recur in the global ledger, as one counted while it was off, is charged on its session's own ledger, so its repair can claim it.
- Changed the harness overview to list only refinements that applied edits and were not periodic turn-interval or compaction rounds.
- Fixed queued refines running in the wrong scope: a local `refine.run` no longer inherits global scope from an earlier request, and a request of the other scope now runs on its own after the queued one (dropped without planning if the session is aborted or changes branch first, but kept through a compaction and planned after it), reported as pending by `refine.status`.
- Fixed a queued refine dropped by an abort or a branch change disappearing silently, including one a non-interactive session (print, JSON, RPC, ACP) was already planning in the background: it now emits `refine_failed`, and a recurrence or regression of the failures that queued it can queue a repair again.
