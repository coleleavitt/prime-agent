- Cron schedules use standard day matching: when both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted, either one firing is enough (`0 0 13 * 5` runs on the 13th and on Fridays); a starred field (`*`, `*/N`) still requires both. Sparse schedules such as leap day (`0 0 29 2 *`) now resolve from any year, and impossible ones (`0 0 30 2 *`) are rejected instead of failing with "did not match within one year".
